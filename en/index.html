CFS

Do you need a CFS?

  1. Horizon Europe: a CFS is required if you claim at least €430,000 of EU contribution as actual costs (cumulative, per beneficiary).
  2. It is submitted with your final financial report. Without it, the final payment is delayed.
  3. Other EU programmes set their own rules – check point 4.3 of your Data Sheet and Article 24 of the grant agreement. Lump-sum grants need no CFS.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When do I need a Certificate on the Financial Statements (CFS)?

Under Horizon Europe a CFS is usually required from €430,000 of EU contribution per beneficiary. The exact duty is in your grant agreement and Data Sheet — we will confirm it with you.

Is a CFS the same as a statutory audit?

No. A CFS is agreed-upon procedures under ISRS 4400 (Rev.). The auditor performs specific procedures set by the grant authority and does not give an opinion on the financial statements as a whole.

What documents will you need from us?

Grant agreement and amendments, financial statements, personnel and other cost records, timesheets and internal policies. We agree the exact list before work starts.

How long does a CFS take?

Typically 2–4 weeks depending on scope and document quality. After the call you get a realistic timeline so the auditor's report meets your deadline.

What do we cover on the first call?

Your grant situation, whether and when you need a CFS, and next steps — with no obligation.

Is the CFS fee an eligible project cost?

Usually, yes. When your grant agreement requires a CFS, its cost is normally an eligible direct cost under Horizon Europe and Horizon 2020 rules (other goods and services). Check the exact conditions in your grant agreement – we are happy to help.
