Grant programme audits and Certificate on Financial Statements (CFS)

Grant agreements with European Commission agencies and other grant authorities often require verification of expenditures. We prepare the Certificate on Financial Statements (CFS) and auditor’s report in compliance ISRS 4400 (Agreed-Upon Procedures).

When is a CFS required?

Under Horizon Europe, a CFS is typically required when the EU contribution for a single beneficiary reaches or exceeds €430,000. For older H2020 projects the threshold was €325,000. The exact obligation always follows from the Grant Agreement and Data Sheet.

What a CFS is — and is not

A CFS is not an audit of financial statements with an audit opinion. It is an agreed-upon procedures engagement under ISRS 4400(Rev.). The auditor performs specific procedures set by the grant authority — and oes not give an opinion on financial statements as a whole.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When do I need a Certificate on Financial Statements (CFS)?

Under Horizon Europe a CFS is typically required when the EU contribution for one beneficiary reaches or exceeds €430,000. For H2020 the threshold was €325,000. The exact obligation is always in the Grant Agreement and Data Sheet — we are happy to check this with you in a free consultation.

Is a CFS the same as a statutory audit?

No. A CFS is an agreed-upon procedures engagement under ISRS 4400. The auditor performs specific procedures set by the grant authority — it is not an opinion on the financial statements as a whole.

What kind of supporting documentation will you need from us?

Typically the Grant Agreement and amendments, financial statements for the reporting period, support for personnel and other costs, timesheets and internal policies. We tailor the exact list to your programme and agree it before work starts.

How long does preparing a CFS take?

It depends on project size, quality of documentation and the reporting deadline, but typically around 2-4 weeks. After the first consultation you receive a realistic timeline; with timely preparation all engagements can meet the grant authority's deadline.

Is the first consultation really free of charge?

Yes. The first in-person or online consultation is free of charge. We discuss your situation, whether a CFS is required and next steps — with no obligation.
